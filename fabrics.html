Know Your Fabric

Fabric Guide

🌡️ Care & Washing

Silk fabrics should be dry cleaned or hand washed in cold water. Cotton can be machine washed. Georgette and chiffon require gentle hand washing. Always iron on reverse side with medium heat.

📏 Fabric Calculation

For a saree: 6–6.5 meters. For a salwar suit: 3–4 meters. For a kurta: 2–2.5 meters. For a lehenga: 4–8 meters depending on design. We help you calculate the exact amount you need.

✂️ Custom Tailoring

We can recommend trusted local tailors who specialise in ethnic wear. Bring your design reference and we'll help you pick the most suitable fabric for your vision.

💼 Bulk Orders
