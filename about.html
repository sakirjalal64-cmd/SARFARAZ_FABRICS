About Sarfaraz Fabrics

Est. 2004, Bhagalpur

Weaving Trust Since 2004

Sarfaraz Fabrics was born from a simple belief — every person deserves to dress beautifully without compromising on quality. Founded in Bhagalpur, Bihar in 2004, we started as a small fabric store with a big dream: to bring the finest textiles of India to the doorstep of our community.

Over two decades, we have grown into one of Bhagalpur's most trusted names in ethnic wear and premium fabrics. Our collection spans everything from handwoven Banarasi silk to contemporary blended fabrics, from bridal lehengas to everyday salwar suits.

What sets us apart is not just the quality of our products, but the relationships we build with every customer. We take pride in personally guiding each visitor through our collection, understanding their needs, and helping them find exactly what they're looking for.

Our Journey

Milestones Over the Years

2004

Founded in Bhagalpur

Sarfaraz Fabrics opens its doors with a small but curated selection of quality fabrics in the heart of Bhagalpur.

2008

Expansion of Collection

Introduced bridal wear and exclusive silk collections, rapidly gaining popularity among Bhagalpur's fashion-conscious community.

2012

New Showroom

Moved to a larger, modern showroom to accommodate our growing collection and increasing number of loyal customers.

2016

5000+ Satisfied Customers

Reached the milestone of serving over 5,000 happy families across Bihar, from Bhagalpur to Patna, Muzaffarpur and beyond.

2020

Digital Presence

Launched online enquiry services and social media presence to reach customers beyond physical boundaries.

2024

20 Years of Excellence

Celebrating two decades of trust, quality, and community. 10,000+ customers and counting!
