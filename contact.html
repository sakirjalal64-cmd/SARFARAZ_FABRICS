Kya aap fabric meter mein bechte hain?+
Haan! Hum sare fabrics meter mein bechte hain. 1 meter se 100 meter tak — sab available hai. Bulk orders par special discount bhi milta hai.
Kya online order aur delivery milti hai?+
Abhi hum primarily physical store hain. Lekin aap WhatsApp par apni requirement share karein — outstation customers ke liye hum shipping discuss kar sakte hain.
Bridal consultation available hai?+
Bilkul! Appointment lekar aayein aur hum aapko poori bridal collection personally dikhayenge. WhatsApp par contact karein appointment ke liye.
Return ya exchange hota hai?+
Uncut, unwashed fabric 7 din mein exchange ho sakta hai original bill ke saath. Ready-made items 3 din mein exchange hote hain. Receipt zaroori hai.
Kya payment methods accept karte hain?+
Cash, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, aur sare UPI apps. Debit/credit card bhi chalte hain. Bade orders ke liye installment bhi discuss ho sakti hai.
